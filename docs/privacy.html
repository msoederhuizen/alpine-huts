Privacy Policy

Alpine Huts · last updated 28 September 2026

The short version. Alpine Huts has no sign-up, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you plan, save and photograph stays on your phone, and your location never leaves it.

There is one exception, and it only happens if you choose it: you can send a photo or a review to be shared with other walkers. Doing that uploads what you wrote or photographed, and creates an anonymous identifier so the contribution can be linked back to you — which is what lets you edit or delete it later. You can delete everything you have sent, at any time, from inside the app.

What stays on your device

These are stored only in the app's own storage on your phone. They are not transmitted, backed up to us, or shared:

  • Photos you add to a hut, from your camera or photo library
  • Notes you write about a hut
  • Routes and trips you plan or save
  • Your settings, such as your chosen region and difficulty ceiling
  • Hut photographs the app has downloaded so they work without a signal

Deleting the app removes all of it. There is no copy anywhere else — unless you have chosen to send something in, which is covered next.

If you send in a photo or a review

This is entirely optional. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to share it, and the rest of the app works exactly the same if you never do.

What is sent

  • The photograph itself, and any caption you write with it
  • Your star rating and any comment you write
  • Which hut it is about, and the date and time you sent it
  • An anonymous identifier created for your device

The anonymous identifier

The first time you send something, the app creates an account for you automatically. It has no email address, no password and no name — it is a random identifier, nothing more. It exists so that your own contributions can be shown back to you and so that you can edit or delete them afterwards. Under data-protection law it still counts as personal data, which is why it is described here.

It belongs to that installation of the app. Reinstalling, or using a second phone, creates a different one.

Where it is stored

Contributions are stored by Supabase, which hosts the database and the photographs on our behalf. They process the data only to provide that service, and do not use it for anything else.

The servers are in Ireland, within the European Union. Your contributions are not transferred outside the EU or the UK.

Nothing appears until it has been checked

Every photograph and review is read by a person before anyone else can see it. Until then it is visible only to you. Something that is not approved is never published.

How long it is kept

Until you delete it, or until the app stops being published — at which point everything is deleted. Something you submitted that was rejected is removed once it has been dealt with.

Why we are allowed to hold it

Because you chose to send it — consent, under Article 6(1)(a) of the UK and EU GDPR. You can withdraw that at any time by deleting what you sent, and doing so does not affect anything else in the app.

Your location

If you allow it, the app asks for your location so the map can show where you are and you can check you are on the right trail. This is foreground only — the app does not track you in the background.

Your position is handed straight to the map to draw on screen. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it is never stored. You can refuse the permission and the rest of the app works normally.

What the app connects to

Alpine Huts ships with its hut data and photo list built in, so most of it works offline. Some features do reach the internet, and when they do, the service on the other end necessarily sees your device's IP address and what was requested — as with any app or web page. We receive none of this.

What we do not do

  • No sign-up, email address or password — and no account at all unless you send something in
  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking of any kind
  • No third-party software development kits that collect data
  • No selling or sharing of personal information, ever
  • No profiling, and no automated decisions about you
  • No reading of your photo library — the app sees only the picture you pick

Children

Alpine Huts is suitable for all ages. Nothing is collected from anyone who simply uses the app. Because anyone who sends in a photograph or review is sharing it publicly, we ask that people under 16 do so only with a parent's agreement.

Your rights

If you have never sent anything in, we hold no personal data about you and there is nothing to look up or erase. The data on your phone is yours: delete individual photos and notes in the app, or remove everything by deleting the app.

If you have sent something in, you have the following rights over it:

  • See it — your contributions are shown to you in the app, including any still being checked
  • Correct it — edit your review at any time; an edited review is checked again before it reappears
  • Erase it — About → Delete everything I have sent removes your photographs, reviews and reports permanently, and Delete my account removes the anonymous identifier with them
  • Object, or withdraw consent — deleting your contributions does exactly that
  • Take it with you — ask at the address below and we will send you what we hold, which will be the photographs and text you sent and their dates

You do not have to ask us to exercise any of these — they are all in the app. If you would rather write to us, or if something is not working, use the contact address below and we will act within one month.

If you think we have handled your data badly, you can complain to your national data-protection authority. In the Netherlands that is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the updated version appears here before the app release it applies to.
